# Product Vision

Detect demand early. Act before the market does.

---

# Product Position

RetailPulse AI is an Applied AI Operational Intelligence Platform for B2B Distribution & Supply Chain.

RetailPulse is an Intelligence Layer—not an ERP.

---

# Problem Statement

Enterprise distributors generate operational data every day inside ERP systems, but existing workflows primarily describe business activity rather than prescribe the highest-value operational actions.

RetailPulse converts ERP records into deterministic operational intelligence for revenue recovery, inventory prioritization and executive decision-making.
