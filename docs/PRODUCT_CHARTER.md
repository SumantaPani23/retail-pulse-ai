# RetailPulse AI — Product Charter

## Purpose

RetailPulse AI turns authorized ERP and operational data into measurable business decisions for B2B distribution and supply chain operations.

## Product Thesis

The ERP remains the system of record. RetailPulse becomes the intelligence layer above it.

## Core Business Outcomes

1. Improve outstanding recovery.
2. Detect revenue leakage early.
3. Identify inventory and replenishment risk.
4. Prioritize actions by measurable INR impact.

## Product Principles

- Deterministic engineering before generative AI.
- Every derived value must be traceable to source data and rules.
- No silent modification of source systems.
- Customer data remains isolated.
- BYOK AI is optional and cannot override deterministic calculations.
- Start narrow, validate with design partners, then expand.

## Initial Customer Profile

Multi-location B2B distributors, wholesalers, and channel partners managing 20–500 retail accounts.

## North Star Metric

**Outstanding Recovery (₹)**

## Supporting Metrics

- Revenue Leakage Prevention (₹)
- Recoverable Revenue identified (₹)
- DSO movement
- Stockout-risk value (₹)
- Action completion rate
- Rule accuracy and false-positive rate

## BETA Boundary

### In Scope

- Manual / CSV data ingestion
- Data validation and normalization
- Outstanding / receivables analysis
- Revenue leakage analysis
- Inventory / stock analysis
- Deterministic prioritization
- Executive dashboard / reports

### Deferred

- Native SAP Business One connector
- Advanced predictive ML
- Autonomous financial actions
- Native mobile application
- Broad multi-industry expansion

### Explicitly Out of Scope

- ERP replacement
- Payment processing
- Unrestricted autonomous agent execution
- Global cross-customer learning without explicit consent
