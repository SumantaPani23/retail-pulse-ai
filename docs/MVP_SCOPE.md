# RetailPulse AI — MVP Scope

## Product Goal

Prove that a distributor will repeatedly use and pay for deterministic intelligence that improves outstanding recovery.

## P0 — Build

- Manual / CSV ingestion
- Data validation and normalization
- Outstanding / receivables analysis
- Revenue leakage analysis
- Inventory / stock analysis
- Priority scoring
- Executive dashboard
- Exportable action list
- Audit trail for every derived output

## P1 — Delay

- Tally-native connector
- SAP Business One integration
- Advanced predictive ML
- BYOK explanation layer
- Expanded event automation
- Multi-company consolidation

## Kill for BETA

- ERP replacement
- Generic chatbot as primary product
- Native mobile application
- Autonomous credit decisions
- Automated payment processing
- Computer vision
- Broad industry modules

## MVP Success Gates

- Real distributor data can be ingested reliably.
- Derived outputs are traceable to source records.
- Priority lists are operationally useful.
- Design partners use the product repeatedly.
- At least one design partner demonstrates measurable improvement in outstanding recovery.
- Willingness to pay is observed through a paid pilot or equivalent commercial commitment.
