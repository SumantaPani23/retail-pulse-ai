# RetailPulse AI

> Detect demand early. Act before the market does.

RetailPulse AI is an **Applied AI Operational Intelligence Platform for B2B Distribution & Supply Chain**.

## Product Position

RetailPulse is **not an ERP replacement**.

It is an operational intelligence layer that reads authorized ERP and operational data, applies deterministic business logic, and turns signals into measurable business decisions.

## Initial Enterprise Customer Profile

Multi-location B2B distributors, wholesalers, and channel partners managing 20–500 retail accounts.

## North Star Metric

**Outstanding Recovery (₹)**

Revenue Leakage Prevention and Inventory Risk are the next intelligence outcomes built around the same core.

## System of Record

- Tally ERP 9 / TallyPrime
- SAP Business One
- Other authorized operational data sources over time

## Intelligence Core

- Data Validation & Normalization
- Deterministic Rule Engine
- Event Engine
- KPI Engine
- Revenue Leakage Analysis
- Inventory / Stock Analysis
- Outstanding / Receivables Analysis
- Priority & Action Engine

## AI Boundary

AI is an optional enhancement, not the financial authority.

A customer-owned **BYOK LLM** may explain, summarize, and contextualize deterministic outputs. It must not override calculations, invent data, or perform unauthorized consequential actions.

## Core Flow

```text
ERP / CSV / API
      ↓
Data Validation & Normalization
      ↓
Deterministic KPI + Rule Engine
      ↓
Revenue Leakage | Inventory Risk | Outstanding Risk
      ↓
Recoverable Opportunity / Priority Queue
      ↓
Executive Intelligence
      ↓
Optional BYOK Agent Explanation
```

## Product Lifecycle

**Discover → Validate → Design Partner → BETA → Measure → Learn → Persevere / Pivot → Scale**

The BETA objective is not feature completeness. It is evidence that distributors repeatedly use and pay for decision-grade intelligence generated from their existing operational data.
